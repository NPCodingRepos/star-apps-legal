Privacy policy

Startell Media app · last updated 27 October 2026 (anonymous learning)

The short version

Startell Media is made to work on your phone. Your videos, your voice, what you say, your posts, your deals and your contracts stay on your device. To make the tips better for every creator, the app sends anonymous counts once a day (for example a score band and whether a tip helped), with no name, account or device ID; you can switch this off in You › Privacy. If you turn on Training partners, your nickname and your training numbers are kept on our server so the friends you invite can see them. We have no ads, and we add no analytics tools of our own.

What stays on your phone

  • Videos you record. The camera and microphone are used only while you film. Clips and finished videos are kept in the app's storage on your phone, and saved to your photo library only when you tap Save.
  • Practice takes and your Star Score. The camera and microphone record a drill only while you film it. The take is measured on the phone: Google ML Kit finds your face to measure eye contact and framing, and an open-source speech model (whisper.cpp) turns your words into text on the device. Takes and the text of what you said are never uploaded. After scoring, the take is deleted, except your day 1 take and your day 28 take, which stay on the phone so you can watch them side by side. Scores, signals and the text of what you said are kept on the phone.
  • Your profile. Your topic, goal, Creator DNA answers and training progress.
  • Reminders. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. There is no push server and nothing is sent to us when one appears. You can switch each one off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Post numbers. Screenshots you pick are read on the phone (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iPhone). The pictures are not uploaded; only the numbers you save are kept.
  • Deals, contracts and your media kit. Contract text is checked on the phone. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Circles. Invites and clips travel through the apps you choose to share with (for example WhatsApp or email). We do not receive them.
  • Duets. The app downloads a list of free clips, and the clip you choose, from our website (npcodingrepos.github.io, hosted by GitHub). Your reply is filmed and joined to the clip on your phone, and saved only when you tap Save. Nothing is uploaded.

Anonymous learning (on unless you switch it off)

Startell learns from all creators which tips, drills, hooks and posting habits work, so the app can coach everyone better. It does this with counts that cannot be tied to you:

  • What is counted. Your topic (one of ten, such as Food or Fitness); for a drill take, the program day and your score and its eight signals in 10-point bands; which tip you were shown and how that point changed on your next take, in 5-point steps; for library drills, the drill and the rep number with a score band; the type of hook and the format of a script you write and of a video you mark as posted; the weekday and 3-hour band in which you mark a video as posted; and, for a post you add to Formula, a views band (for example 1,000 to 3,000), the network, the length band, the opening and the format.
  • What is never sent. No name, handle, account, device ID or advertising ID; no videos, audio, photos or screenshots; no transcripts, scripts or any text you write; no exact dates or times; no post numbers other than the views band.
  • How it is sent and kept. The counts wait on your phone and are sent at most once a day, at a random time, in random order, to our server (a Google Cloud function in the EU, in our Firebase project). The server checks that each count is exactly in the shape above, adds it to running totals (how many, sum and sum of squares) and keeps nothing else: no single record is stored, and the server does not keep request logs or IP addresses for it.
  • What comes back. Once a day the app downloads the totals as a small public file. A number is only included when at least 100 records are behind it, and a little random noise is added. The app uses it to pick the tip that helped creators most, to order ideas, hooks and suggested drills, to show when creators post, and to show how creators like you score.
  • Switching it off. Open You › Privacy and turn off Anonymous learning. Counts waiting on the phone are deleted and nothing more is sent. Counts already added to totals cannot be taken out, because nothing in them points to you.

Training partners and duels (optional)

Training partners is off until you turn it on. When you do, the app creates an anonymous account (a random ID, with no email or phone number) and keeps a card on our server (Google Firebase, Cloud Firestore). The card holds your nickname, your STAR score and level, your six skill scores, your medal counts and number of mastered drills, your streak, and your points for the current week. Only people who have your invite link can open your card; there is no search and no public list. Your list of partners and the people you blocked stay on your phone.

In a duel, the person who starts it and the person who answers share their nickname, their score for the drill and its eight signal scores. Duels are deleted from the server 14 days after they start. Videos are never sent: each person's take is scored on their own phone.

If you report a partner, the app opens an email to us with their nickname and card code. We use it only to look into the report.

To delete your data, open Training partners and tap Turn off training partners. This deletes your card, the duels you started and your anonymous account from the server. Cards that are not updated for 180 days are deleted automatically. You can also email support@startell.com.

Diagnostics from Google ML Kit (Android)

On Android, face detection and text reading use Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone. ML Kit sends Google anonymous diagnostics about its own use: device model and Android version, the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you, speed and error codes. It does not send your pictures, videos or text. Google uses this to keep ML Kit working and does not pass it to third parties. See ML Kit's data disclosure.

The speech model download

The first time you score a take, the app downloads the speech model (about 57 MB) once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public file host. Like any website, Hugging Face sees your device's IP address and the file request. No video, audio or text of yours is sent. See Hugging Face's privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check which plan is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user id and the store's purchase records (product, dates, price, country). It does not receive your videos, your name or your contacts. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Who we work with

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) store the Training partners cards and duels, only if you turn the feature on, and the anonymous learning totals. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • RevenueCat checks which plan is active (above).
  • Hugging Face and GitHub host files the app downloads (the speech model, the duet clips). They see your IP address, like any website.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps about you is on your phone, unless you turned on Training partners (anonymous learning totals hold nothing about you). Delete what is on your phone by removing the app, or in your phone's settings clear the app's storage. To delete your training partner card, your duels and your anonymous account, open Training partners and tap Turn off training partners (or email us). To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us and we will delete them.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over. See our child safety standards.

Changes and contact
