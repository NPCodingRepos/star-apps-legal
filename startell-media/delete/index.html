Delete your data

Startell Media app (developer: Startell Media) · last updated 23 October 2026

Training partners: delete it yourself, in the app

If you turned on Training partners, open it and tap Turn off training partners. This deletes at once from our server your card (nickname and training numbers), the duels you started and your anonymous account. Duels someone else started are deleted automatically 14 days after they start, and cards not updated for 180 days are deleted automatically.

Or ask us by email

  1. Email support@startell.com with the subject Delete my Startell Media data.
  2. If you bought a plan, add the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt (it starts with GPA. on Google Play). It lets us find your purchase record, because the app has no accounts. For a training partner card, add your nickname.
  3. We confirm by email once it is done, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Training partners data on Google Firebase: your card, your duels and your anonymous account.
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat (the service that checks which plan is active): the random app user id and the purchase history linked to it. We delete this customer record.

What stays, and why

  • Everything on your phone (practice takes, scores, Creator DNA, posts, deals, contracts) never reaches us. Delete it yourself: uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.
  • Billing records at Google Play or the App Store are kept by Google or Apple under their own rules and tax law. We cannot delete them.
  • Anonymous diagnostics from Google ML Kit (Android) are not linked to you, so they cannot be matched to a request. Google keeps them under its own retention rules.
