Startell Creators

By Startell Media · last updated 14 November 2026

What it is

Startell Creators connects online stores with creators. A store sets a reward and approves creators. A creator applies, gets a personal link and a coupon code, and earns a reward on orders that come through them. Startell tracks the orders and shows the numbers to both sides. Startell never holds or transfers money: store credit is issued by the store's own platform, and cash is paid by the store directly to the creator.

For stores

  • WooCommerce plugin. Install it, click Connect, choose the reward (store credit, cash, or both), the buyer discount and whether to approve creators automatically.
  • Shopify app. The same setup inside your Shopify admin.
  • Only orders that came through a creator's link or coupon are reported to the service. Buyers' billing emails are never sent.

For creators

Creators use the Stores tab inside the Startell Media app: find a store, apply, share your link and code, and follow your visits, orders and earnings.
