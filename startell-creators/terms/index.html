What the service does

Startell Creators lets a store offer a reward to creators who bring it orders, and lets creators find stores, apply, and track what they earned. The service gives each approved creator a link and a coupon code for the store, matches orders to them, calculates the reward and shows it to the store and to the creator. You may use it for personal and business purposes if you follow these terms and the law.

We never hold or move money

  • Startell does not hold, collect, pay out or transfer any money between a store and a creator. We only track orders and show what is owed.
  • Store credit is issued by the store's own platform (a coupon in WooCommerce, store credit in Shopify).
  • Cash is paid by the store directly to the creator. The store marks it as paid in the admin screen.
  • The deal about rewards is between the store and the creator. Startell is not a party to it and is not responsible for a store's or a creator's failure to pay or to honour it.

How orders are credited

  • A visitor who opens a creator's link is remembered for 30 days (a first-party cookie, sc_ref, on the store's own domain). If an order uses a creator's coupon code, that creator is credited. Otherwise, if the visitor came through a creator's link, that creator is credited.
  • The reward is a percentage of the order amount after discounts, without shipping and tax. The store sets the percentages.
  • A new reward is held for 30 days, or the hold the store sets, and then approved. If an order is refunded the reward is reduced, and if it is fully refunded or cancelled the reward is cancelled.
  • An order is not rewarded when it appears to be made by the creator themselves. We detect this by comparing a one-way hash of the buyer's billing email with a hash of the creator's contact email.
