Gridmoor: Daily Logic Puzzles: Privacy Policy

Short version

Gridmoor: Daily Logic Puzzles collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on this device.

One setting from our server

When the app starts it reads one public setting (a single word that says whether the app opens with a free trial or as a free app) from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The request carries no account and no data about you; Google receives your IP address as with any request. Nothing else leaves your phone.

Purchases

Gridmoor Pro is sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or account details. The app asks the store which subscription is active; there is no third-party subscription service.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn them on, the phone shows a daily nudge at the time you choose, and one at 20:00 when today's puzzles are not finished. They are scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Turn them off in Settings.

Your control

Settings > Reset all progress removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Terms of Use

Subscriptions

Gridmoor Pro renews automatically every week or year, depending on the plan you choose, until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. The price shown is the price the store charges. Both plans start with a 7-day free trial; if you do not cancel before it ends, the plan you chose is billed.
