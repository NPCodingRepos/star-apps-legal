Jigsaw Atelier: Art Puzzles: Privacy Policy

Short version

Jigsaw Atelier: Art Puzzles collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on this device.

Your photos

When you make a puzzle from your own photo, you pick it with the system photo picker. The app reads only that photo, keeps a resized copy in its own storage on this phone and never uploads it. Delete it in the app or by uninstalling the app.

Pictures from the internet

Museum search sends the words you search for to The Metropolitan Museum of Art's public Open Access service and loads the pictures from it. Studio drops are loaded from a small public catalog we publish on GitHub Pages. Those services see your device's internet address, as every website does; we receive nothing.

Purchases

The Atelier Pass is sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or account details. The app asks the store which subscription is active; there is no third-party subscription service.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

Settings > Delete all my data removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Terms of Use

Subscriptions

The Atelier Pass renews automatically every month or every year, depending on the plan you choose, until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. The price shown is the price the store charges.

The pictures

Collection pictures are public-domain works from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access program and NASA, credited on each puzzle. NASA does not endorse this app. Studio drops are our own photographs, licensed to you for use in the app. You keep all rights in your own photos and confirm you have the right to use them.
