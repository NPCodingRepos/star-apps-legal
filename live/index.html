Matchvel: Live Scores & Tennis: Privacy Policy

Short version

Matchvel: Live Scores & Tennis collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your favourites and settings stay on this device.

Where the scores come from

Scores, schedules and match statistics come from two data providers: Highlightly (football, basketball, hockey, American football, baseball, rugby, cricket, volleyball, handball and other sports) and Live Tennis API (tennis). Our own server collects this data and publishes it to Google Firebase (Realtime Database, servers in the European Union). The app reads it from there. The app never talks to the providers directly, and nothing about you is sent to them. Like every web request, the connection to Google Firebase includes your device's internet address, which Google processes on our behalf under its Firebase terms; we do not receive or store it.

Your favourites

The players, teams and matches you follow, and your settings, are kept on this device only. We cannot see them.

Notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications, alerts such as a tight finish, a tiebreak or match point are created on the phone from the scores it already has, and the phone may show a reminder before a free trial ends. They are scheduled on the phone; nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings or in the phone's settings.

Purchases

Matchvel Pro is sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or account details. The app asks the store which subscription is active; there is no third-party subscription service.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

Settings > Delete all my data removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to delete on our side.
