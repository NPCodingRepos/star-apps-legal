Sharpwell: Daily Brain Training: Privacy Policy

Short version

Sharpwell: Daily Brain Training collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on this device.

Purchases

Sharpwell Pro is sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or account details. The app asks the store which subscription is active; there is no third-party subscription service.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn them on, the phone shows a daily nudge to do your workout. They are scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Turn them off in Settings.

Your control

Settings > Reset all progress removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Contact

Startell Media, support@startell.com. Policy date: 2026-11-13.

Terms of Use

Subscriptions

Sharpwell Pro renews automatically every week or year, depending on the plan you choose, until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. The price shown is the price the store charges. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial; if you do not cancel before it ends, it is billed for the year. The weekly plan has no free trial.

Use of the app

The app is provided as is. Startell Media keeps all rights in the app's design, code and original artwork.
