Scoretide: Live Scores & Stats: Privacy Policy

Short version

Scoretide: Live Scores & Stats collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on this device.

Scores

To show scores and tables, the app loads public match data directly from OpenLigaDB (api.openligadb.de) and from the openfootball files on GitHub. Those requests contain only which competition and season to load, plus your device's internet address as every web request does. We run no server of our own and receive nothing.

Your teams and your games

Favourite teams and the games you score yourself are kept on this device only.

Purchases

The Scoretide Pro is sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or account details. The app asks the store which subscription is active; there is no third-party subscription service.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

Settings > Delete all my data removes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app does the same.
