STAR AI Photo & Video Maker: Privacy Policy

Short version

STAR AI Photo & Video Maker works on your phone. Your photos and videos are processed on the device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect, sell or share personal data.

What the app uses

Photos you choose with the system photo picker. The app reads only the pictures you pick. It saves your results in the app's own storage and, when you tap Save, in your photo library. Nothing leaves the device unless you use the Share button yourself.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see your card or Apple/Google account details. The store tells the app which plan is active.

On-device AI

All AI runs on your phone: depth for the 3D effects (Depth Anything V2 Small, Apache-2.0), object removal (MI-GAN, MIT), enhance (Real-ESRGAN, BSD-3-Clause), and subject cut-out by the phone's own system (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android) with U2-Net (Apache-2.0) as a fallback. On Android, Google Play services may download the cut-out model the first time; your photo stays on the device. The AI writer uses the language model built into your phone (Apple Intelligence on iPhone, Gemini Nano through Android AICore): your text and facts are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to anyone else; on Android the phone may download the model from Google once, handled by the system. On phones without such a model the app uses its own built-in writer. AI results can be imperfect, so check them before you publish.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control

Delete any creation inside the app, or use Settings > Delete all my data to remove everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Pictures you saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them.

Contact

Terms of Use

Subscriptions

Paid plans renew automatically every month until cancelled. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Play Store subscription settings, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The price shown in the app is the price the store charges, including any taxes it adds.

Your content

You keep all rights in the photos and videos you make. You are responsible for having the right to use the pictures you import and for following the rules of the places where you publish them.

No guarantee of results

Automatic tools can make mistakes. Check every picture and video before you publish it.
